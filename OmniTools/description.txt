Two cheap craftable quality-of-life items. The Omni Scepter maxes out life and mana, grants every useful buff and reforges all your weapons and accessories to their best prefixes on left click; right click hands out an endgame gear kit plus every boss summoning item. The Omni Globe shakes up a random personal climate around you - snowfall, sandstorm, blood moon, mushroom glow or hallow - which changes spawns, biome checks and weapon effects without touching the rest of the world.
